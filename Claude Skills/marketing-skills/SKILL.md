# Marketing Skills

40+ specialized marketing skills for conversion optimization, content, SEO, paid ads, analytics, and growth strategy. Built for technical marketers and founders.

## When to invoke this skill

- Writing landing page copy or optimizing for conversions
- Creating email sequences, cold outreach, or social content
- Running an SEO audit or building programmatic SEO pages
- Setting up analytics tracking or A/B test frameworks
- Developing paid ad campaigns or ad creative
- Planning launch strategy, pricing, or content strategy
- Building referral programs or community marketing
