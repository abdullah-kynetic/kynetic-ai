# Deep Research Skill

Multi-source research with citation tracking, evidence persistence, and structured report generation. Produces comprehensive, cited research reports — not quick lookups.

## When to invoke this skill

- Asked to "deep research", "comprehensive analysis", or "research report" something
- Comparing X vs Y in depth
- Trend analysis or state-of-the-art reviews
- Any request requiring 10+ sources and a formal report structure
- **Do NOT invoke for:** simple factual lookups, debugging, single-question answers

## Modes (selected based on complexity)

| Mode | Phases | Time | Use when |
|------|--------|------|---------|
| Quick | 3 | 2–5 min | Initial exploration |
| Standard | 6 | 5–10 min | Default |
| Deep | 8 | 10–20 min | Critical decisions |
| Ultradeep | 8+ | 20–45 min | Comprehensive reviews |

## Workflow phases

1. **SCOPE** — Define research boundaries and assumptions
2. **PLAN** — Create investigation strategy
3. **RETRIEVE** — Gather sources (phases 3–5 loop per section)
4. **TRIANGULATE** — Cross-verify evidence across sources
5. **SYNTHESIZE** — Build analysis from verified evidence
6. **CRITIQUE** — Challenge findings, identify gaps
7. **REFINE** — Iterate and strengthen weak sections
8. **PACKAGE** — Generate final report

## Required report structure

- Executive Summary (200–400 words)
- Introduction with methodology and assumptions
- Main Analysis (4–8 findings, each with citations)
- Synthesis & Insights
- Limitations & Caveats
- Recommendations
- Complete Bibliography
- Methodology Appendix

## Quality standards

- Minimum 10 sources with cluster-independent diversity (3+ per major claim)
- Every factual statement cited immediately
- No fabricated citations — ever
- 80%+ prose composition (not bullet-point dumps)
- No placeholders or "TBD" in final output

## Core principle

Operate independently. Infer assumptions from context. Only stop for critical errors or incomprehensible queries.

## Works alongside

`tavily-mcp` (web search), `firecrawl` (page extraction), `context7` (technical documentation).
