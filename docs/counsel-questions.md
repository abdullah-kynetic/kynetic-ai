# Questions for counsel (tasks 1.4 and 4.8)

Prepared Oct 7, 2026 to hand to the lawyer contacts (via Quentin). These are questions, not legal advice. Context: Kynetic AI sells an AI voice agent to home service businesses (first niche: roofing). The agent calls a lead within about 60 seconds of that lead submitting the business's own web or Meta lead form. We do not place cold AI calls (D-007).

## Product facts counsel needs
- The lead submits a form that includes consent wording and a checkbox. We store the consent text, timestamp and lead source.
- The agent opens with: "Hi, is this [name]? This is [agent], an AI assistant calling from [company] about the form you just submitted. This call may be recorded. Is now an okay time?" (D-011). It never denies being an AI.
- Voicemail: the agent hangs up. Retries at about 5 minutes, 1 hour and next day, maximum 4 attempts (D-012).
- The agent can book an appointment on the business's calendar. No SMS in v1 (A2P is not registered; voice first, D-004).
- Kynetic AI is the vendor. The roofing company is the client and the caller of record.

## Questions
1. **Consent for AI voice calls:** What consent wording does the lead form need so a callback by an AI voice agent is covered under the TCPA? Is "prior express consent" enough, or do we need "prior express written consent"? Please supply approved wording.
2. **Who is the caller:** Does liability sit with the client, with Kynetic AI, or both? What should the agreement say about indemnity and AI liability (task 4.7)?
3. **AI disclosure:** Federal and state rules on disclosing that a caller is an AI. Is our opener sufficient? Any state where it differs?
4. **Call recording:** Our opener says the call may be recorded. Which states need all party consent, and is that statement enough for each? Roofing leads may be anywhere.
5. **Calling hours and frequency:** Federal and state limits. We plan up to 4 attempts over about a day. Any state with tighter rules (for example time windows or call counts)?
6. **Do not call:** Do we need to scrub the national and state DNC lists for form submitters who gave consent? What opt out must the agent honor on the call, and how fast must it be recorded?
7. **Lead source risk:** Forms we do not build (the client's own Meta lead ads). What do we need from the client to prove consent (screenshots, wording, timestamps)?
8. **Emergency branch gap (KI-002):** Emergency calls and urgent notifications may reach someone who opted out. What is the correct handling and wording? This applies to the inbound receptionist (Plan 2) but the opt out logic is shared.
9. **Agreement (4.7, 4.8):** Review the MSA and SOW for the single outbound offer: AI liability section, TCPA responsibilities, results guarantee wording, data handling.
10. **Solar:** If we later add solar, any extra rules we should know about before we build it.

## Ask of counsel
A written answer to each question, approved consent wording for the form, and review of the agreement. Please say which items need a state by state check.
