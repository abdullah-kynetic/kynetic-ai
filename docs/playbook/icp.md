# Kynetic AI ideal customer profile (ICP)

Approved by Abdullah on 2026-11-16. This file is the source of truth; update it here by branch and PR.

## Who can take the free AI audit

Any business owner in the US. The audit is free and automated, and it is the opener on every channel: cold calls, email outreach and the website.

## Who we sell to

**Niches (outbound dialing lists):**
- Remodelers (design-build, kitchen, bath)
- Solar and battery installers
- Foundation repair and basement waterproofing

**Business:**
- Owner-led. The owner can be hands-on or not, as long as they want to grow.
- The owner, or a partner who can sign, makes the buying decision.
- The owner is reachable on their own cell. If we cannot find the owner's cell, the business is parked, not dialed through the front desk.

**Location:** anywhere in the US except Florida and Oklahoma.

**No ads or marketing filter.** Running ads is a good signal but not required. Referral-only and no-marketing businesses qualify.

## Excluded

- HIPAA businesses (dental, medical)
- AM-1 Roofing (do not call)
- Corporate chains, franchises run from headquarters, and private-equity-owned groups
- Any business with no reachable owner

## Outside the niches

Inbound audit leads outside the three niches that score high on our 9 problems are still worked the same way. Solutions are tagged by the problem they solve, not by industry.
