## Front-desk rule
- Goal: find the business owner's own number. Businesses with a front desk are NOT dropped (Abdullah, Oct 13). Dial the owner's direct number where one is found; otherwise use the number on the business's own website.
- On calls: if staff answers, ask once for the owner by name, then ask for a callback, and log "front desk." Never pitch staff.

## Florida / Oklahoma
Businesses based in FL or OK are not prospected. This is a prospecting exclusion, NOT a call-time rule (Alex has no FL/OK calling rule; Abdullah declined it). Marked for lawyer review in the sales rules.
