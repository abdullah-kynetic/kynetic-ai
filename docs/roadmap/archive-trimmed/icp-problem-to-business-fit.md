Formalizes the ICP from first principles: what problem each system solves, what kind of business has that problem, then the qualifying gates.

**7-gate ICP criteria (adopted Aug 2026, gate 7 added):**
1. TAM - enough businesses in category + region to sustain outreach volume (~300 conversations needed for 3 clients)
2. Ticket size - average job value high enough that the revenue-leak math clears $2,500/mo
3. Under ~20 employees
4. Digitally invisible - weak or no real website, thin/inconsistent GBP presence
5. Inbound dependent - leads arrive via calls/forms/ads, not pure referral or bid-based B2B
6. Year-round demand, not sharply seasonal
7. Field-based workforce - owner/staff physically away from the phone/desk most of the day (strongest predictor of missed-call volume, the single strongest signal in the whole set)

**Disqualifiers:** franchise locations with corporate call centers, businesses with a full-time office manager and active review process already, high-volume low-ticket categories (e.g. lawn mowing), pure e-commerce/storefront retail with no phone intake, sharply seasonal-only categories.

**Current niches (Sep 2026, narrowed from 8 to 2):** Restoration (Water/Fire/Storm) - passes cleanly on every gate, insurance-funded which removes budget objection, ~60,000-65,000 US businesses, ICP fit 9/10. Plumbing - passes cleanly on every gate, ~125,000-130,000 US businesses, ICP fit 7/10, commonly-targeted niche but closes a real TAM gap.

Net addressable ICP-fit pool across both niches: roughly 111,000-117,000 businesses nationally. TAM is not the binding constraint, call volume and close rate are.
