## Objection Handling Guide (original)

Use these word-for-word, do not improvise on objections, these are tested.

**"What are you selling?"** -> "I'm not selling anything on this call, I do a free audit walkthrough. You'll see exactly what I found, and if it's not relevant to you, that's totally fine."

**"I'm not interested."** -> "Completely fair, can I ask, is it that you're not interested in the audit, or you're just slammed right now? Because the numbers I pulled are specific to your business, I'd hate for you to miss what I found."

**"I already have someone who handles my marketing."** -> "This isn't really a marketing play, it's a revenue leak. Missed calls after hours, overflow, weekends. Most marketing agencies don't touch that. The audit just shows you the gap, no obligation."

**"Send me an email."** -> "I can do that, but honestly what I found doesn't translate well to an email. It's a 20-minute call and you'll see the full picture. When's a good time this week?"

**"How much does it cost?"** -> "The audit is completely free. If there's something I can help with after, we can talk about that, but that's a separate conversation."

**"We already have a system for that."** -> "That's good to hear, what are you using? Because most systems I've seen don't cover the after-hours and overflow gap. That's usually where the leak is. The audit would show you if that's true for your business."

**"Call me back later."** -> "Absolutely, when's a better time? I'll put it in my calendar right now."

## Merged from: Cold Call Script (Audit Angle) (original note c0dfb1d3, archived Oct 13, 2026)

Purpose: book the audit call only. No pitch, pricing, or demo on this call.

**One liner:** "We find the revenue [niche] contractors are losing to missed calls, and plug the leak with an AI system that works 24/7." Swap the niche word every call.

**30 Second Cold Open (Primary):**
"Hey [Name], I'll keep it short. I got about 30 seconds to explain why I'm calling. You want to hear it or should I just kick rocks?" (They almost always say go ahead.)
"Appreciate it. My name's Abdullah. I work with [niche] contractors. Before I called, I ran a quick audit on [Business Name]. I pulled your Google profile, checked your competitors, and estimated what you're probably losing in missed calls every month. Found a gap worth showing you. I'm not pitching anything, I just do a free 20 minute walkthrough of the numbers. You free [day] to jump on a quick call?"
Total time under 30 seconds. If yes, book it and get off the phone.

**Permission Bridge** (if asked "what's this about"): "Totally fair. I run a free business audit for [niche] contractors. I pulled your Google profile, looked at your call volume, and checked your top 3 competitors. Found a gap that's probably costing you jobs every month. Takes 20 minutes to walk through, and you'll know the numbers whether you work with me or not."

**The Hook** (if still on the fence): "Here's what I found. [Business Name] is missing roughly [X] calls a day after hours and on weekends. At your average job ticket, that's somewhere around $[number] a month going to whoever picks up first, easily [Y]% of the leads you're already paying to generate. That's usually a competitor." (Pause. Let it land.)

**The Ask:** "I've got time [tomorrow/Thursday]. Does morning or afternoon work better?"

Notes: cold call goal is to book the audit call only, demo happens on the audit call not here, website is never mentioned, tailor the one liner by niche on every call.

## Merged from: Audit Call Framework (original note e55a57e5, archived Oct 13, 2026)

Purpose: deliver audit findings, tie down the pain, run the revenue calculator, demo the live agent, close to proposal. Structure runs 45 minutes.

**[0-5 min] Set the Agenda:** "By the end of this call I want to show you three things, where calls are leaking out of your business, what your competitors are doing that you're not, and what fixing it looks like live. Sound good?"

**[5-15 min] Discovery** (core 5, pull more from the Audit Question Bank if time allows): How are you currently handling calls after hours and on weekends? What happens when a lead calls and no one picks up? How many jobs do you think you're missing in a typical week? What's your average job ticket? How are most of your leads coming in right now?

**[15-20 min] Audit Findings** - present 3 findings: GBP health (rating, review count, posting frequency vs top competitor), competitor gap (who's outranking them and why), revenue leak estimate (missed calls x average ticket x 30 days). "Based on what you just told me, that's roughly $[number] a month walking out the door, or about [Y]% of the leads you're already paying to generate. That's the leak."

**[20-25 min] Tie-Downs:** "If you could fix that, if every call got answered, every lead got followed up, and your Google profile was actively working for you, what would that be worth to the business?" Let them answer. "And right now, none of that's happening automatically, right?"

**[20 min] Obstacles check (C.L.O.S.E.R. - O):** "Before I walk you through what fixing this looks like, is there anything else I should know? Anyone else who'd need to be involved in a decision like this, or anything from a past vendor or agency that soured you on this kind of thing?"

**[25-35 min] Live Agent Demo + Pitch Deck:** walk findings with the per-prospect pitch deck, then "I want to show you something live. I'm going to call a number right now, this is the same system I'd install for your business." Call the demo number, let it answer, walk through what happened. "That call was answered in under 2 seconds. It qualified the lead, asked the right questions, and would have booked that job automatically. That runs 24/7, nights, weekends, overflow, while you're on another job."

**[35-45 min] Close to Proposal:** "Based on what you've told me, this is exactly what I'd build for [Business Name]. I'll put together a one-page proposal, specific to your business, your numbers, and we can get on a call in the next day or two to walk through it. Does [day] work?"

Notes: never price on this call, the demo is the close mechanism (test it before every call), let them say the ROI number, never say it for them.

## Merged from: Audit Question Bank (original note b7ef41ed, archived Oct 13, 2026)

Full structured diagnostic list behind the Audit Call Framework's Discovery segment. The framework's core 5 questions stay as the default script for a 45-minute call, pull from here to go deeper.

**1. Call Handling & Lead Capture:** How are you currently handling calls after hours and on weekends? What happens when a lead calls and no one picks up? How many jobs do you think you're missing in a typical week because of a missed call? What's your average job ticket? Do you have a system that texts a missed caller back automatically? When someone fills out a form on your website, how long before they hear back? How are most of your leads coming in right now?

**2. Online Reputation & GBP:** How many Google reviews do you have right now, and what's your rating? When's the last time you posted an update to your GBP? Do you ask every customer for a review? Do you respond to reviews? Have you looked at your top 2-3 competitors' profiles recently?

**3. Follow-Up & Nurture:** When a lead doesn't book right away, what's your follow-up process? How many times do you typically follow up before giving up? Do you have an easy way to reach back out to old customers or cold leads? If someone no-shows, what happens next?

**4. Current Systems, Staff & Tools:** Who's responsible for answering calls and texts right now? Are you using any CRM or scheduling tool today? If your best office person called in sick tomorrow, what happens to your calls that day? Have you tried any automation or AI tool for this before, what happened?

**5. Growth Goals & Decision Process:** What would it mean for the business if you captured even half the leads you're currently missing? What's the 12-month goal, more jobs, bigger jobs, less of your own time on the phone? Besides you, is anyone else involved in a decision like this? Has an agency or vendor tried to sell you something like this before, how did it go?

Note: the last two questions overlap the Obstacles check in the Audit Call Framework, skip if already covered.

## Merged from: Proposal Call Framework (original note afa335f4, archived Oct 13, 2026)

Purpose: walk through the proposal, handle final objections, close. Structure runs 30 minutes.

**[0-5 min] Frame the Call:** "I sent over the proposal, did you get a chance to look at it? Perfect. I want to walk through it quickly, answer any questions, and we can figure out if it makes sense to move forward."

**[5-15 min] Walk the Proposal, in order:** 1) the problem, restate their revenue leak number from the audit call; 2) the solution, walked system by system (Reputation Engine, AI Speed-to-Lead Agent, Lead Recovery & Nurture), tying each back to their stated problem, asking "what questions do you have on that" after each; 3) the deliverables; 4) the investment (setup fee + monthly retainer); 5) the timeline (live in 7 days).

**[15-25 min] Handle Objections:**
"It's too expensive." -> "I get that, let's put it in context. You said you're losing roughly $[number] a month in missed calls. The retainer is $2,500. If this system captures even two of those jobs back, it's paid for. Everything after that is margin."
"I need to think about it." -> "Totally fair, what's the main thing you're weighing? Is it the investment, the timing, or something about the system itself?"
"I need to talk to my partner." -> "Makes sense, when are you talking to them? I can jump on a quick call with both of you if that helps move it faster."

**[25-30 min] Close (C.L.O.S.E.R. - N, Narrow the Gap):** "On a scale of 1 to 10, how ready do you feel to move forward with this?" Anything under a 9: ask what's stopping a 10, handle it, re-ask before moving on. "Based on everything, does this make sense to move forward?" Wait for their answer. If yes: "Perfect. I'll send over the agreement and invoice today. Once that's done we'll book the kickoff call and get started."

Notes: send proposal within 24 hours of the audit call, proposal expires in 5 business days (state this on the call), the two-call close is intentional, it filters serious buyers from tire kickers.

## Merged from: C.L.O.S.E.R. Framework Reference (original note 1bc6b562, archived Oct 13, 2026)

Reference checklist, not a replacement script. The Audit Call and Proposal Call frameworks are the scripts actually run. Maps C.L.O.S.E.R. onto them so nothing gets skipped.

C - Comfort: rapport then take the authority seat. Audit Call [0-5 min]. Covered.
L - Learn: numbers-first discovery, find the gap. Audit Call [5-15 min]. Covered.
O - Obstacles: "anything else I should know?" Audit Call, before findings. Applied Aug 2026.
S - Stakes: cost of staying stuck, ask permission to walk through it. Audit Call [20-25 min tie-downs]. Covered.
E - Explain: system by system, assumptive "what questions do you have on that". Proposal Call [5-15 min]. Covered.
N - Narrow the gap: 1-10 readiness, find and handle what's under a 9. Proposal Call, before close. Applied Aug 2026.
G - Get paid: next steps stated, then price, then silence. Proposal Call [25-30 min close]. Covered.
