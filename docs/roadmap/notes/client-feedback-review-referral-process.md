Triggered sequence, not a one-off ask. Manual is fine for the first 1-10 clients.

**Trigger map:** contract signed -> Thank You email/text within 1 hour. Go-live day -> personal thank-you call or Loom video. 30 days post-go-live -> first Monthly Report + check-in. 60 days, first clear win -> ask for a review/testimonial referencing the specific win. 90 days / renewal -> send Referral Ask. Any unprompted positive comment -> immediately ask permission to use it as a testimonial.

**Review ask script (60-day):** "[Name], since we booked your AI Receptionist live, it's caught [X] calls that would've gone to voicemail before, [X] of those turned into real jobs. If you've got 2 minutes, would you mind leaving us a quick review? [Review link]."

**Referral ask script:** "[Name], glad this is working for you. Know any other [niche] owners who are missing calls or sitting on a dead lead list? We'll credit you 30% of their setup fee once they sign on."

**Referral incentive (decided Sep 5, 2026):** 30% of the referred client's setup fee ($900 intro / $1,350 standard), paid once the referral signs, pegged to the setup fee not the retainer.

## Merged from: Client Feedback Doc (original note 285c1ea2, archived Oct 13, 2026)

Short, low-friction check-ins, not a long survey.

**Cadence:** 30 days post-go-live - 3-question check-in. 90 days post-go-live - full feedback form (5-7 questions). Ongoing - open channel for the client to flag anytime.

**30-Day Check-In:** 1) scale of 1-10, how's the system working so far? 2) what's one thing that's clearly working? 3) what's one thing you wish was different?

**90-Day Feedback Form:** overall satisfaction (1-10), which system delivered the most value, has the AI Receptionist ever handled a call in a way that felt off, noticed a measurable change in leads/bookings/reviews, would you refer another [niche] business to us.

**Routing:** score 8-10 + positive comment -> flag for Review & Referral Process. Score 5-7 -> log as a watch item, schedule a check-in call. Score 1-4 or any specific complaint -> create a Task immediately, don't wait for the next report cycle.
