# Sales Call Rules (from scripts.md, commit 7c1830a, Oct 13 2026)

Hand-dialed by humans only. No AI cold calls, no cold SMS, no ringless voicemail. Owners only.

## Before every dial
- Check the sheet for do-not-call (Bath Masters is DNC).
- Dial the number from the business's own website.

## Who answers
- Owner: run the opener.
- Staff: ask for the owner by name. If the owner isn't available, ask for the owner's direct number or cell, or the best time to reach them, plus a callback. Log it as "front desk, callback" and redial. Front-desk businesses stay on the list. Never pitch staff.
- "Don't call again": apologize, end the call, mark do-not-call.

## Discovery: disqualify if any apply
- Fewer than 30 leads a month from all sources combined (Meta, Google, website form). No ad-spend minimum.
- Average job under $5,000.
- Owner not on the call.
- Based in Florida or Oklahoma (a prospecting exclusion, not a call-time rule) [FOR LAWYER REVIEW].
- A franchise or a lead-gen reseller.
- No room or crews for 4+ more estimates a month.
- Won't add TCPA consent wording to the lead form before go-live (Kynetic supplies it; wording TBD by counsel).
- Bought or cold lead lists.
- Wants guaranteed bookings or revenue.

## Also ask
- "When a new form comes in, where does it go?" A webhook, email or Zapier is fine. A form that only lands in an inbox someone checks by hand gets marked "setup needed at onboarding." That's not a disqualifier.
- Close rate is a discovery question, not a disqualifier. If they don't know it, the calculator uses 20%, labeled as a placeholder.

## Close
Invoice 1 by Stripe, plus a kickoff booking.

## Call log outcomes
Who answered (owner / staff / unknown), "front desk, callback," callback requested yes or no, do-not-call.
