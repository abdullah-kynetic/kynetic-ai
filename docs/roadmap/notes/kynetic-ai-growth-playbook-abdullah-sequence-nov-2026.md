# Kynetic AI — Growth Playbook (Abdullah's sequence)

Built from Tim Chow's course, adapted to our vision and locked to Abdullah's order of operations (Nov 5, 2026).

## North star
- Positioning: AI growth partner. Main site = broad vision. Niche pages = specific pitch (remodelers first).
- Sell outcomes/results. Systems and automations are how we deliver.
- ICP: high-ticket, owner-run / small-team, Meta ads, US except FL/OK. No HIPAA.
- Offer ladder: free audit → applicable system (Alex / speed-to-lead entry) → optional 1–3 day on-site Full AI Overhaul (+$5–10K) → monthly partnership.
- Twenty is the only system of record. No GHL. Compliant SMS. No income-claim ads.

## The sequence (do not reorder)

### Track A — Productize in parallel (starts now, never blocks sales)
Build the rest of the systems and automations on **demo clients**, plus reusable templates, so a new signed client can be stood up in **a couple of hours** from the template. Remaining work is optimization only (**up to a couple of days**).

Includes:
- Portal: client + admin complete, auth locked, onboarding checklist, Alex/billing/docs/imports/webhooks.
- Template pack per system: Speed-to-Lead (Alex), Follow-Up & Lead Recovery, Reputation, Ops/Back-Office, Custom AI & CRM, Full Overhaul.
- n8n + ElevenLabs / Twilio / Cal / Twenty wiring as copy-and-configure, not rebuild.
- Demo-client records used for dry runs; never touch live Alex `agent_8201…`, Nora, Emily, or Kynetic Restoration (Demo) production records without a yes.
- Success check: "new client → live in ~2 hours from template; polish ≤ 2 days."

### Track B — Finish sales machine, then cold call only
1. Finish the portal (deploy the auth fix; admin must not be public).
2. Perfect the pitch: audit script, close script, Dream 100 one-pager, objection handles, action-based guarantee wording, pricing ladder numbers.
3. Site: strip live Remodelers TODOs/placeholders; main site stays broad; Remodelers is the booking/niche page.
4. **Cold call only** until **3 clients are signed**. No Meta, no mass SMS, no mass email, no content engine as a primary channel yet.
5. Cold-call rules: remodeler ICP, no FL/OK, AM-1 Roofing do-not-call, DNC scrub, quiet hours. Two-call sale (Abdullah call 1 / Quentin signs). Book call 2 before call 1 ends (≤5 days). Record with consent → Twenty. Scope Stage 1 / Overhaul / partnership.

### Track C — After 3 signed clients: open other channels (lowest barrier first)
Order of expansion:
1. **Compliant cold SMS** (opt-in / TCPA-safe path only).
2. **Content** on Instagram, X, LinkedIn (demo clips, scope breakdowns, founder videos — outcomes only, no revenue claims).
3. **Cold email** (Lead Leak Audit free hook).
4. Then build the **full lead-finding → scrape → nurture → fulfillment pipeline** in Twenty (list building, enrichment, sequences, handoff to delivery templates from Track A).
5. Meta / paid ads only after proof (case studies) and preferably after the pipeline above is working — Tim's rule: outbound before ads.

### Track D — Delivery that sells Stage 2
- First 48 hours: portal login, intake, one visible win (Alex on their leads).
- Same-day response; portal is status of record.
- Day 30 optimization call → pitch Full AI Overhaul (on-site option).
- Capture named case card + testimonial for Remodelers page.
