# Plan 1 proposal template

Internal draft for review. Copy for a specific client only after eligibility and scope
are confirmed. This template does not send a proposal or authorize a deployment.

## Parties and qualification

Record the client legal name, authorized signer, business state, service area,
project types, current lead sources, time zone, operating hours and calendar owner.
Reject Florida- and Oklahoma-based businesses. Do not use Kynetic Restoration
(Demo) or AM-1 Roofing as onboarding/test records.

## Proposed service

Kynetic configures an outbound speed-to-lead assistant for the client's permissioned
inbound leads. It collects project type, budget, ownership and timeline, then offers
calendar availability and records the confirmed outcome. Email is optional for the
lead; never invent an email to satisfy a booking form. A booking is confirmed only
after the calendar provider reports success. No guarantee of revenue, a booking
count or an exact response time is implied by a stub demonstration.

Deliverables: signed lead intake; tenant isolation; server-side consent, opt-out,
quiet-hours and kill-switch gates; client-specific calendar rules; outcome/retry
tracking; reviewed writeback; and the selected notifications. Notification delivery
is conditional on channel approval and testing; do not promise SMS before A2P and
consent requirements are satisfied. Do Not Call registry integration is deferred
under current project scope; internal opt-outs remain enforced.

## Commercial schedule

Use the client-approved quote as the source of truth. Historical Plan 1 pricing in
[D-017](../decisions.md) is $4,675 for the first three clients and $8,500 thereafter,
paid 50% at start, 25% when built and sent for testing, and 25% at completion.
It is not a subscription. Verify eligibility and the signed amount before issuing
anything; do not infer the client's position from synthetic CRM records. Plan 2,
new agency Stripe pricing, discounts and recurring fees are outside this template.
See [invoice procedure](invoice.md) for the reference installment arithmetic.

## Responsibilities and acceptance

The client supplies documented lead consent, approved wording and recording
requirements, accurate business/calendar settings, authorized access and an owner
for exceptions. Kynetic supplies configuration, tests and operator handover. Changes
to live agents, numbers, workflows, DNS or production deployment need explicit
approval; a signed commercial proposal alone is not operational go-live approval.

Attach the [agreement draft](contract.md), [onboarding checklist](checklist.md)
and a written list of included/excluded modules. Record the review date and signer.
Do not add a guarantee, refund policy, support SLA, data-retention period or legal
jurisdiction unless the parties have agreed it. Those terms must be completed in
the agreement before sending it for signature.
