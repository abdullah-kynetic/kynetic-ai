# Service agreement drafting checklist

Internal agreement template, not an executed contract. Complete the commercial and
legal schedules with the parties' approved terms before external use. Counsel review
of consent, recording, AI disclosures and responsibility allocation remains an
acceptance gate; this document does not certify legal compliance.

## Agreement sections to complete

1. **Parties and scope.** Identify legal entities, authorized signers, effective date
   and the accepted Plan 1 proposal. List the specific lead sources, calendar,
   voice assistant and notifications included. Exclude cold outreach, Plan 2 and
   any unstated agency feature. Florida/Oklahoma businesses are ineligible.
2. **Fees and milestones.** Insert the signed price and 50/25/25 milestone amounts
   when applicable, plus any expressly agreed taxes, third-party usage costs and
   payment dates. No automatic subscription or price change is authorized.
3. **Client inputs.** Client provides accurate hours/time zone/service area,
   ownership of the authorized numbers and accounts, consent provenance and
   approved contact/recording wording. Never use undocumented consent as a basis
   to send. Registry checks remain a deferred feature, not a completed safeguard.
4. **Kynetic obligations.** Configure the agreed integration, protect credentials,
   isolate tenants, retain opt-out handling and send gates, record outcomes and
   provide operating instructions. Do not represent a stub booking as a real one.
5. **Access and data.** Describe permitted processors, account ownership, roles,
   purpose of processing, retention/deletion requests, export and incident contacts.
   Fill these schedules from the parties' agreed policy; do not invent a duration.
   Keep keys in the approved secret store, never in the signed agreement.
6. **Acceptance and changes.** Define the agreed test and the client's acceptance
   contact. Require separate approval for live calls/SMS, workflow activation,
   production changes and any additional scope. A failed gate blocks sending.
7. **Support and limits.** Agree support hours, escalation, remedies, liability
   allocation, cancellation, refunds, renewal (if any), governing law and disputes.
   Do not insert an unapproved guarantee or default legal jurisdiction.
8. **Signatures.** Include the final schedules, proposal/version references,
   signers' names/titles and dates. Keep the executed artifact in the authorized
   client workspace; retain an audit reference rather than duplicating secrets.

## Execution gates

The proposal, agreement and invoice must describe the same amount and scope.
Open commercial/legal fields block signature; they do not block synthetic code tests.
Do not send or sign this draft, charge a card, create Twenty records, change a live
agent or activate a workflow as part of completing the documentation check.
Use [checklist](checklist.md) to track review and [workspace](workspace.md) for
provisioning boundaries. Owner approval for the step-6 live test is still required.
