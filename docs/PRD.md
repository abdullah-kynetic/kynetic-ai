- Product: Kynetic Client CRM & Portal. A custom-coded CRM and client portal sold to Kynetic's home-service clients, plus a Kynetic admin side that runs every client from one view.
- Owner: Abdullah (decision maker). Quentin is sales lead and pre-go-live tester (project memory, Oct 20, 2026).
- Status: Decisions approved by Abdullah on 2026-10-25 (see "Decisions log"). Items still marked UNRESOLVED need outside information and are listed at the end of that section.
- Files: the docs live in `/mnt/project-files/kynetic-crm-docs/docs/` (PRD, DESIGN_SYSTEM, ARCHITECTURE, DATABASE, SECURITY, CODE_STYLE, TESTING). AGENTS.md is at `/mnt/project-files/kynetic-crm-docs/AGENTS.md`. In the build repo: `AGENTS.md` at the root and the seven docs in `docs/`.
- Problem: Alex (the AI voice agent) calls new leads within seconds, qualifies them and books estimates, but results are scattered across Retell/ElevenLabs, Twilio, n8n and Twenty. Clients cannot see what Alex did or what it earned against their starting numbers, and Kynetic cannot onboard or monitor many clients cheaply.

- Desired outcome: Every Alex result lands in the client's own CRM and is visible against a signup baseline from day one. Onboarding a client takes about 30 seconds. Each Kynetic system or upsell is a separately sellable module that is switched on or off per client.
- Locked product decisions (from DECISIONS_locked.md):
  - Client data lives in Postgres with row-level security (RLS) per tenant. Twenty is never used for client data; it stays Kynetic-internal.
  - Base is the Kiranism next-shadcn-dashboard-starter (MIT). Clerk is dropped. Atomic CRM (MIT) is a data-model and screen reference. AGPL CRMs (Twenty, EspoCRM, SuiteCRM, Frappe, NocoBase) are reference only, never forked.
  - Runs on Abdullah's own server in Docker with Cloudflare in front.
  - Every Kynetic system and upsell has its own backend and is toggled per tenant with feature flags.
  - v1 branding per client is logo, color and name. Texting inbox and per-call cost visibility are Later.
  - Backups: 30 days plus 12 monthly, with a monthly restore test.
  - Deploys are preview-only until Abdullah approves.

## User journeys
Each journey: actor, trigger, steps, result, failure and recovery.

1. **Lead to booked estimate (Alex).**
   - Actor: a homeowner lead, Alex, client staff.
   - Trigger: a lead arrives on `/api/hooks/leads/:tenantKey` (form, Angi, Google LSA, Facebook).
   - Steps: signed token verified, contact and deal created in the default pipeline, consent checked, Alex calls inside the tenant's call window, outcome and transcript return by webhook, a booked estimate creates an appointment inside business hours and moves the deal to Estimate Booked, staff are notified.
   - Result: the booking is on the calendar and the timeline, and the dashboard counts it.
   - Failure and recovery: duplicate webhook is a no-op (unique external id). No consent, outside call window or DNC means no call is placed and the reason is written to the timeline. Provider error retries through the job queue, then shows in integration health.
2. **Owner checks results.**
   - Actor: client owner.
   - Trigger: opens the portal (usually on a phone).
   - Steps: dashboard stat cards against baseline, guarantee tracker, drills into a call, plays the recording, flags a bad call for Kynetic review.
   - Result: owner sees ROI and can give feedback.
   - Failure and recovery: empty state shows the next action (for example "No calls yet. Alex starts when the first lead arrives"). A flagged call appears in Kynetic's review list.
3. **Staff works the pipeline.**
   - Actor: client staff.
   - Trigger: a new-lead notification.
   - Steps: opens the contact, reviews timeline, adds a note, creates a task, drags the deal between kanban stages, marks a task done.
   - Result: every write appears on the timeline.
   - Failure and recovery: a failed drag rolls the card back and shows a toast. Moves and edits have undo.
4. **Kynetic onboards a client in about 30 seconds.**
   - Actor: Kynetic admin.
   - Trigger: client signs.
   - Steps: one form (business name, owner email, phone, industry template, hours, service area, logo and color, plan), tenant created with owner invite, default pipeline, default automations, Alex config draft, module toggles set.
   - Result: the owner receives an invite and a working tenant exists.
   - Failure and recovery: the form is transactional, so a failure leaves no half-created tenant. Duplicate slug or email is rejected with a clear message.
5. **Admin supports a client (view-as).**
   - Actor: Kynetic admin or VA.
   - Trigger: client reports an issue or health score turns red.
   - Steps: opens All clients, uses view-as (read-only by default, banner visible), checks integration health.
   - Result: the issue is diagnosed. The session is in the audit log.
   - Failure and recovery: a VA attempting a write while impersonating, or an action reserved for admin, is refused and logged.
6. **Admin turns a module on or off for a client.**
   - Actor: Kynetic admin.
   - Trigger: client buys or cancels an upsell.
   - Steps: opens the tenant, toggles the module, saves, change is audited.
   - Result: module UI appears or disappears, and server routes and jobs for that module start or stop.
   - Failure and recovery: with the module off, direct API calls to it return 403 and queued jobs no-op. Data is kept, not deleted.
7. **Lead texts STOP.**
   - Actor: a contact.
   - Trigger: inbound SMS containing STOP (or HELP).
   - Steps: Twilio inbound webhook verified, consent revoked, active sequences for that contact stop, confirmation logged on the timeline.
   - Result: no further calls or SMS to that contact on that channel.
   - Failure and recovery: a replayed webhook is a no-op. If processing fails, it retries and the contact is blocked from sending until processed (fail closed).
8. **Monthly ROI report.**
   - Actor: system, client owner.
   - Trigger: month end.
   - Steps: the rollup job builds the report against baseline, emails it (PDF or email), posts a "report ready" notification.
   - Result: owner reads results without logging in.
   - Failure and recovery: a failed send retries, and Resend bounces appear in integration health.

9. **New client starts with their existing data.**
   - Actor: Kynetic admin.
   - Trigger: client signs and sends an export from their current tool (customers, leads, open jobs and quotes, history).
   - Steps: admin opens the tenant, uploads the file, maps columns, runs a dry run, reads the report (failures, duplicates), fixes the file or mapping, commits, checks the contacts list.
   - Result: the portal opens with the client's real customers and open jobs, each tagged with source and batch. No calls or texts were started.
   - Failure and recovery: a bad batch is undone in one action. A failed commit leaves a listed, undoable partial batch.

### Metric definitions (F-01, F-49; decided 2026-10-25, approved as written)
- Leads received: count of contacts created through lead intake in the range (imports excluded, F-96).
- Speed-to-lead: median seconds from lead creation to the first outbound call attempt. Shown against the baseline captured at signup.
- Calls made: count of outbound call attempts in the range.
- Contact rate: leads with at least one answered call (outcome not voicemail or no answer) divided by leads received.
- Estimates booked: count of appointments with status scheduled, completed or no-show created in the range.
- Booking rate: estimates booked from leads received in the range divided by leads received.
- Revenue recovered: sum of `value_cents` of deals that moved to a Won stage in the range and whose contact had an Alex call or an Alex-booked appointment.
- Baseline: values captured at signup in `tenants.baseline` (same metric names). Definitions may be refined by Abdullah; changing one requires updating the M6 fixtures.

## Success measures
- First founding client live and using the portal daily, with Alex bookings recorded and a monthly ROI report delivered (spec v1 success). Measurement: production `metrics_daily` and `audit_log` for that tenant. Target: achieved at M10.
- Tenant isolation: 0 cross-tenant reads or writes in the automated suite. Measured in CI, target 100% pass, release-blocking.
- Onboarding time: under 30 seconds from submitting the form to a working tenant. Measured by an end-to-end test and timestamps on the audit rows.
- Module gating: with any module off, 0 successful calls to that module's routes (test per module). Release-blocking.
- Webhook idempotency: replay of the same event 10 times produces 1 effect. Measured in tests.
- Import: 1,000 rows with column mapping imports successfully (spec M9 test).
- Backups: nightly job success rate and a passing monthly restore drill (spec M10: restore drill must pass before pilot).
