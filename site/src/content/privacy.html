Privacy Policy

Kynetic AI. Effective Date: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what we collect on kyneticai.com and through our services, why we collect it, who we share it with, how we share it, and how we protect it. Kynetic AI is the business name of Abdullah Ahmed, a sole proprietor based in Irving, Texas ("Kynetic AI", "we", "us").

1. What we collect

1.1 When you book an audit call. You book through our scheduling tool, cal.com. We collect your name, email address and mobile phone number, the date and time you book, and whether you agreed to receive text messages and when you agreed.

1.2 From our clients. If you hire us, we collect business contact details for the people who work with us, billing information, and the information and access we need to build and run your systems.

1.3 Client call and lead data. Our AI receptionists and lead follow-up systems handle calls, messages and lead details (such as a caller's name, phone number and what they asked for). We process this data for the client we built the system for, under our agreement with that client. We do not use it for our own marketing.

1.4 Payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card or bank account numbers. We keep only what Stripe gives us for our records, such as invoice details and payment status.

1.5 From your browser. Basic website usage data, such as pages visited, collected through standard analytics.

2. How we use it

2.1 To schedule and hold your audit call.

2.2 To send the text messages about your booking described in Section 4.

2.3 To reply to you when you contact us.

2.4 To build, run and support our clients' systems, and to invoice and collect payment.

2.5 To understand how our website is used and to improve it.

2.6 To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

3. Who we share it with, and how

3.1 We do not sell your personal information.

3.2 Service providers. We share information only with the providers that help us run Kynetic AI and only as needed for that work. These include Stripe (payments and invoices), Cal.com (scheduling), Google, Cloudflare, Twilio (text messaging), and ElevenLabs (voice AI), along with other email, hosting and AI providers we use in client builds.

3.3 How we share it. Information goes to these providers through their own secure systems, such as their dashboards and software connections, over encrypted connections. We do not send personal information by unsecured means.

3.4 When the law requires it. We may disclose information when required by law, a court order or a legal process, or to protect our rights.

3.5 Mobile phone numbers and text consent. We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

4. Text messages (SMS program: Kynetic AI Booking Notifications)

4.1 You will only get texts from us if you check the SMS consent box when you book. Checking it is optional, and you can still book without it. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

4.2 We send about 1 to 3 messages per booking: a confirmation, a reminder, and any change to your booking. We do not send marketing or promotional texts.

4.3 Message and data rates may apply.

4.4 Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. You can also email abdullah@kyneticai.com or call (908) 477-9490.

4.5 Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

5. How we protect it

5.1 Our website and the tools we use send data over HTTPS, so it is encrypted in transit.

5.2 Access to personal and client information is limited to the owner, Abdullah Ahmed.

5.3 We protect our accounts with strong passwords and two-factor authentication.

5.4 We rely on reputable providers that follow industry-standard security practices.

5.5 No system is perfectly secure. We use reasonable safeguards, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. How long we keep it

6.1 We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires, such as for tax and accounting records. Client call and lead data is kept as our agreement with that client says.

7.2 If you are a caller or lead of one of our clients, please contact that business first about your information, since we handle it on their behalf. We will help them respond to your request.
