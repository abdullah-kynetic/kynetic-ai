Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains how Kynetic AI (sole proprietor Abdullah Ahmed) collects and uses your information when you visit kyneticai.com, book an audit call with us, or receive booking text messages from us.

Information we collect

  • Your name, email address and mobile phone number, which you enter when you book an audit call.
  • Your booking date and time.
  • Whether you agreed to receive text messages, and when you agreed.
  • Basic website usage data, such as pages visited, through standard analytics.

How we use it

  • To schedule your audit call with Kynetic AI.
  • To send text messages about your booking: a confirmation, a reminder, and any change to the time. We don’t send marketing or promotional texts.
  • To reply to you when you contact us.

Text messages

You’ll only get texts from us if you check the SMS consent box when you book. Checking it is optional, and you can still book without it. We send about 1 to 3 messages per booking. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We share information with service providers that help us run our services, such as our booking and messaging providers, only as needed to provide those services.

Retention and your choices

We keep your information only as long as we need it for these purposes. You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information at any time.
