FAQs

Straight answers

What does the free audit cover?
A 30 minute walkthrough of how your business runs. You leave with a list of where jobs, time and money slip away and what each leak costs you every month, whether or not you work with us.
What kinds of tasks can your AI systems handle?
Anything repetitive that costs you jobs or time: calling leads back, following up on quotes, booking, reminders, review requests, reaching old customers and back-office admin. Alex, our speed-to-lead caller, is live today. The rest is built for you after your audit.
What results can I expect, and how fast?
Alex is live within 14 days. Once live, Alex calls new leads back within 60 seconds and books the appointment on your calendar. We build, run and monitor it for you, and your client portal shows every booked job. What you get depends on your lead volume and on your team answering the leads we book.
Will this add work for my team?
No. We build, run and monitor it for you, and nothing is switched on until you agree. Your team’s part is answering the leads we book.
How much does it cost?
Start with a free audit; you keep the leak list either way. The first fix starts from $6,500, for example Alex, live in 14 days. A full AI overhaul is scoped on your audit, and the monthly partnership covers running, monitoring and improving everything. Financing is available, so you can start with little or nothing down.
