## What we build
An AI speed-to-lead agent for **[Business name]**:
- Every new lead from **[lead source]** gets a phone call within about 60 seconds, day or night.
- The assistant introduces itself as an AI assistant calling for [Business name], qualifies the lead, and books a **[free inspection]** on your calendar.
- If nobody answers, it tries again at 5 minutes, 1 hour and the next day (4 attempts at most).
- Every call, recording, transcript and booking is logged for you.
- Your lead form carries consent wording, so every lead has agreed to be called.

## Not included in Plan 1
- Inbound call answering, review requests, and old-lead reactivation. These are add-ons for later.
