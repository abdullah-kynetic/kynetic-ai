# Roofing Company Knowledge Base

## Company overview

The company is a family owned and operated roofing company based in Bend, Oregon. It has served Central Oregon since nineteen ninety-three and was incorporated in nineteen ninety-five. Thomas and Jennifer Hunziker are the owners. The company does both residential and commercial work.

## Pitched and steep roofs

For pitched and steep roofs, the company installs asphalt shingles, metal roofing, synthetic roofing, ceramic and concrete tile, and wood. For shingles it works with CertainTeed, including CertainTeed Landmark shingles.

## Flat and low-slope roofs

For flat and low-slope roofs, the company offers cold-applied asphalt systems, roof coatings, low-slope metal, PVC, and T P O. It has installed T P O for more than twenty years. It also installs G E Enduris high solids silicone roof coatings and Duradek PVC walkable deck membranes.

## Repairs and inspections

The company does roof repairs, including leaks, missing or damaged shingles, cracked tiles, flashing around chimneys and vents, and flat roof ponding water. It can also help with sagging roofs, condensation, and roof ventilation problems. Routine roof inspections, repairs, and new roof appointments can be scheduled with the office.

## Inspection and estimate visit

The roof inspection is free. The visit is at the property and takes about thirty minutes. The inspector looks at the roof and the problem, and a written estimate follows after the visit. The exact cost depends on the job, and the inspector covers it during the visit.

## Other services

The company also does siding, gutters, sheet metal work, and steel buildings. Siding includes wood, metal, and cement board, for new construction and remodels. Gutters include seamless systems, repairs for gutters and downspouts, and both residential and commercial jobs. The company runs a full-time sheet metal shop where it makes metal roofing, siding, chimney caps, and flashings, and custom sheet metal orders usually take a couple of days. For steel buildings, the company works with you from the first consultation through final completion, is a preferred Varco Pruden builder, and uses American-made steel.

## Residential and commercial

The company works on both homes and commercial buildings, from small repairs to large projects.

## Warranties and certifications

The company is a CertainTeed Shingle Master Applicator, listed by CertainTeed at the ShingleMaster Premier level. It says it is one of only two contractors in Central Oregon certified to offer the CertainTeed workmanship warranty. Its website says it offers a warranty for as long as you own your home on certain CertainTeed installs. The exact terms depend on the roofing system, so the inspector or the office confirms what applies to a given roof. The company installs G E Enduris silicone roof coatings, which have a manufacturer coating warranty of up to twenty years, depending on the system. The company is also a GAF GoldElite Commercial Contractor, which applies to commercial roofing systems.
